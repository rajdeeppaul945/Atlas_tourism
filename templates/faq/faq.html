Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions regarding booking procedures, payments, inclusions, and trip cancellations.

Booking is effortless! Simply browse through our Destinations or Packages section, click on Book Now for your preferred holiday, pick your desired travel date, enter the number of adult and child travellers, fill in traveller details, and complete your checkout using Razorpay or test demo mode. Once payment succeeds, your confirmed booking invoice is generated immediately.

We use industry-standard Razorpay payment gateway integration supporting UPI, NetBanking, debit/credit cards, and digital wallets. We never store raw card or banking credentials on our servers. All transactions are end-to-end encrypted with SSL.

Yes. Cancellations initiated more than 15 days before your travel date are eligible for a 90% refund. Cancellations made between 7 to 14 days receive a 50% refund. To request a cancellation or change of departure date, please visit your Dashboard > My Bookings or contact our customer desk.

Each package has a dedicated Inclusions & Exclusions breakdown on its details page. Typically, accommodation in verified hotels, private or shared ground transportation, daily breakfast, and sightseeing entry assistance are covered. Personal shopping, flight tickets (unless specified), and optional adventure activities are excluded.

During the booking checkout step, you will see a coupon input field. Enter promo codes like EXPLORE15 or ATLASNEW and click Apply. The discount percentage will immediately recalculate your grand total.
